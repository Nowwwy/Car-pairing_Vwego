This basic fallback omits buildings, labels, footpaths and most land features outside
the original Pangyo extract. It does not provide turn-by-turn navigation or live traffic.
Whole OSM ways crossing the query bounds can extend beyond those bounds.
No road coordinates are interpolated or invented. Map data corrections:
https://www.openstreetmap.org/fixthemap

Online OSM raster tiles are fetched only for the viewed map. Viewed tiles are cached
for at least seven days and older cached tiles may remain visible during a reconnect.
There is no raster-tile prefetch, downloadable-region feature or bundled raster archive.
